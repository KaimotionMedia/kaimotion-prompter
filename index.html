How to use

  1. Script: paste your script, or open a .txt or Word (.docx) file.
  2. Choose Voice (the script follows you as you talk) or Auto (it scrolls at a set speed).
  3. Press Start. For Voice, allow the microphone the first time.
  4. Read the line on the band. Ad-lib, pause or skip a line: it waits and catches up.
  5. Tap or click any word to jump there. Drag or use the mouse wheel to scroll by hand.

Teleprompter glass: use Mirror. "Flip left-right" is the usual setting for a beam-splitter rig. Use "Flip upside down" or "Both" if your screen sits the other way.

Keys: Space = start/pause · ↑ ↓ = nudge a line · ← → = slower/faster · Page Up/Down = a screen · Home = top · M = mirror · F = fullscreen · + / − = text size. Presentation clickers work too.

Voice follow works in Chrome, Edge and Safari (including iPhone and iPad), not Firefox. Chrome and Edge need an internet connection for it; Auto mode works offline.
