How to use

  1. Edit: write, paste or import (.txt or Word .docx) your script. It saves as you type, and you can keep several scripts on this device.
  2. Choose Voice (the script follows you as you talk) or Auto (it scrolls at a set speed).
  3. Pick the language: Auto guesses English or Spanish from the script; or choose one. Your choice wins.
  4. Press Start. For Voice, allow the microphone the first time.
  5. Read the line on the band. Ad-lib, pause or skip a line: it waits and catches up.
  6. Tap a word to jump there. Double-tap a word to edit the script right there; press Prompt to come back to the same spot.

Spanish and English in one script: put [ES] on its own line before the Spanish part and [EN] before the English part. The prompter switches the listening language when the reader gets there. It listens to one language at a time.

Phones: hold the phone sideways. On Android, Start goes full screen and locks it sideways. iPhone can't lock from a web page, so just turn it.

Teleprompter glass: use Mirror. "Flip left-right" is the usual setting for a beam-splitter rig. Use "Flip upside down" or "Both" if your screen sits the other way.

Keys: Space = start/pause · ↑ ↓ = nudge a line · ← → = slower/faster · Page Up/Down = a screen · Home = top · E = edit · M = mirror · F = fullscreen · + / − = text size. Presentation clickers work too.

Voice follow works in Chrome, Edge and Safari (including iPhone and iPad), not Firefox. Chrome and Edge need an internet connection for it; Auto mode works offline.
